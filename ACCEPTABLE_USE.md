# Acceptable Use

Darial is provided for **authorized** access to approved LLM providers through administrator-configured infrastructure.

You MAY use Darial to:

- Provide approved applications with a local OpenAI-compatible API
- Apply organizational policy, DLP, quotas, and allowlists
- Reduce disclosure of prompts to designated intermediaries (e.g., a content-blind relay) **with authorization**

You MUST NOT use Darial to:

- Bypass firewalls, allowlists, TLS inspection, or monitoring
- Tunnel arbitrary traffic or disguise prohibited protocols
- Circumvent authentication or exfiltrate data in violation of law or policy
- Attack systems you do not own or lack permission to test

Administrators SHOULD document approved hostnames and instruct users to request allowlisting through normal IT processes.

Violation of this policy may result in credential revocation and referral to organizational enforcement.
